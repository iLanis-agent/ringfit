RingFit
Buying a ring from another country? The size on the tag is a different measurement.
Open the app
A US size 7 is ISO 54: 54.35 mm around, 17.3 mm across. In UK letters that is N 1/2.

What it does

  • US, UK letters, ISO and Swiss/Italian/Spanish sizes side by side
  • Start from a ring that fits (inner diameter) or your finger circumference
  • Shows circumference and diameter in mm and inches
  • Nearest half UK size with its exact length
Formulas and the ISO table from Wikipedia, Ring size (ISO 8653:2016). Jeweller charts can differ by half a size. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source

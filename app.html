RingFit

Convert a ring size between US, UK, ISO and Swiss systems, or start from your finger or a ring that fits.
Sources: Wikipedia, Ring size. ISO 8653:2016 size is the inner circumference in mm. US = (circumference - 36.5) / 2.55. UK letters step 1.25 mm with C = 40 mm, in half sizes. Swiss/Italian/Spanish size = circumference - 40. Charts from jewellers differ by up to half a size, and fingers swell with heat and time of day. Indian, Japanese and Chinese scales are not linear and are not converted. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
